2. What is Feed to Worm Casting Conversion Rate?

This is the estimated worm casting mass after chicken feed is eaten twice. Once by the chicken and excreted as manure, and the manure then eaten by insects and excreted as worm castings.

3. Why is it called a Chicken Fortress?

It's a steel box with extreme heat shielding, a 55 gallon water drum, a 50 pound feed hopper, and a vermiculture pit that turns manure into protein for the chickens. It is the most siege resistant chicken coop ever made.

4. Can I lock the doors and use the Chicken Fortress as a self-cleaning battery cage?

No. The automatic doors are corner positioned so that the chickens will nest on the opposite wall. Once the doors are closed and locked, those two corners become a new favorite nesting spot and you won't be able to collect eggs from the maintenance room anymore.
